In normal natural and social evolution, the system is set up so that**the better-adjusted, more talented, more ethical, and more productive person**has a better chance of surviving, reproducing, building a family, raising healthy children, and passing on their values ​​to future generations. This is the point of evolutionary selection and of any functioning civilization.

In the system described in this manifesto,**the direction of the team is reversed**. Here**the more talented one is punished more severely than the more mediocre one**because:

* the more talented**rejects corruption schemes**and therefore he is financially, administratively and judicially harassed (5.5.1—5.5.5);  
    
* the more talented**thinks independently**and therefore it is silenced in the public sphere (5.5.7);  
    
* the more talented**stands out**and therefore he is rejected from the competition tables (5.5.6);  
    
* the more talented**criticizes**and therefore he is persecuted as a criminal (5.5.8);  
    
* the more talented**seeks the truth**and therefore it is blackened and erased from memory (5.5.9);  
    
* the more talented**refuses to break**and therefore he is ultimately deprived of access to medicines that would keep him alive (5.5.10).

The result of this system on a generation is aging, impoverishment, and premature mortality.**The result over several generations is something else entirely: a systematic reduction in the proportion of talented people in the population.**

#### *Is it "intentional" the wrong question?*

In civilized discussion, the question is often asked: “Is this reverse selection intentional?” This question is wrong because it assumes that a central intention is necessary for the result to exist.**Biology doesn't work like that.**

One thing is enough:**the system rewards mediocrity and punishes talent**— and reverse selection happens automatically, without anyone centrally directing it. Just as natural selection is not “directed” by nature, but simply**arises from environmental conditions**.

The Bulgarian environment of the last 30+ years**is set directly against the talented person**— financial, administrative, medical, media, competitive, demographic. The result —**24 per 1,000 mortality rate, lowest fertility rate in Europe (around 1.5), massive brain drain for generations**— it's no surprise. This is**mathematically inevitable outcome**from such an environment.

#### *Why this is more terrible than “genocide” in the classical sense*

Classic genocide—that of the 20th century—is direct, visible, recorded. It has perpetrators with names and uniforms, dates of mass killings, numbers of victims. It is horrific, but**at least it is recognizable**.

Reverse selection by slow cooking is**more cynically destructive**because:

* **there is no single day of the disaster**— there has been a 30-year gradual decline;  
    
* **no visible perpetrators**— there are thousands of “officials who follow the procedure”;  
    
* **there are no mass graves**— there are statistical graphs;  
    
* **no executioners**— has “regulators”;  
    
* **there is no formal ban on existence**— there is a slow impossibility to live, to work, to be born, to dream.

And that's exactly why**the victims were never officially declared victims**. Their names are not written on monuments. Their children receive no reparations. Their unborn grandchildren remain unborn quietly, with no statistical trace except the graph of the declining population.

**This is the modern civilization tool for removing uncomfortable layers**— not of an entire nation (which would be noticed internationally), but of**a specific stratum within the people: the talented, the independent, the ethical, those who cannot be bought**.

#### *Why recognizing this fact is crucial*

Without acknowledging that the ten types of brewing described work as**single reverse selection mechanism**, any partial reform is doomed. The financial pressure (5.5.1) can be fixed — the administrative (5.5.2) will compensate for it. The media space can be opened (5.5.7) — the competition tables (5.5.6) will remain closed. Access to medicines can be restored (5.5.10) — the reversed repression (5.5.8) will continue to punish those who want it.

**Reform must attack the very direction of the team**— not the individual instruments. It must ensure that:

### 6.1. Archive everything — SHA-256 is stronger than an oath

Traditional logic says, “Keep a copy, just in case.” The new logic is different:**every document must be forensically verifiable**— with a date, with a hash, with a verified online mirror.

The practical minimum:

* **SHA-256 hash**on each file, calculated at the time of receipt/download — before anything can be edited;  
    
* **Wayback Machine snapshot**(web.archive.org/save/) on any public page, decision, article, or registry entry — at the time of reading;  
    
* **EPUB/PDF export**from EPEP/EISS with metadata for date and time of download;  
    
* **Email archive with headers**(not just the body) — the header lines Received:, Date: and Message-ID: are tamper-proof, unlike the body;  
    
* **Local log file**with a timestamp for each action: when a document was sent, when an incoming number was received, when a deadline expired.

When these materials are properly archived, the court does not receive an “allegation”—it receives**verified timeline**, which is mathematically harder to challenge than witness testimony.

### 6.3. The language of power — how to talk to institutions without getting lost

The most common mistake of the talented but inexperienced citizen is**the emotional address**: “it's unfair to me”, “they need to stop”, “please”. These phrases are powerless not because they are untrue, but because they leave the assessment entirely to the institution.

The language of power is**structural, verifiable and impersonal**:

* **Instead of**: “I think the judge is biased.”  
  **Write**: “In Ruling No. X of date Y, page Z, the judge officially reflected ‘past relations’ with the plaintiff’s procedural representative, after which she refused the challenge — Art. 22, para. 1, item 6 of the Civil Procedure Code.”  
    
* **Instead of**: “PIE is illegal.”  
  **Write**: “The seizure notice dated Y, line X, contains a threat of criminal prosecution of bank employees — an action outside the comprehensive powers under Art. 16 of the LPEA and Art. 442a of the CPC.”  
    
* **Instead of**: “They didn’t inform us properly.”  
  **Write**: “A printout from the EPEP \[Annex X, SHA: …\] shows the publication of a protocol from a court session with zero content — contrary to the requirements of Art. 131a of the Civil Procedure Code.”

**The rule**: every statement must point to a specific document, a specific order, a specific legal norm, and a specific consequence. When everything is verifiable,**the burden of proof is shifted**— instead of you having to prove that something happened, the institution has to explain why the documents indicate otherwise.
