## 1. WHAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN IN PHYSICS (the honest baseline)

Standard QM gives unitary evolution (Schrödinger) **plus** a measurement rule (Born probabilities, apparent "collapse"). The **measurement problem** is the unresolved tension between the two: unitary evolution never produces a single definite outcome, yet experience records single outcomes. This is genuinely open; competing positions are *legitimate* here:

| Position | Collapse is… | New physics? |
|---|---|---|
| Many-Worlds (Everett) | not real (all branches persist) | no |
| Copenhagen / standard | a primitive postulate | no |
| QBism / relational | an observer's *information update* | no |
| Decoherence (Zurek) | *apparent* (einselection of pointer states) | no (explains appearance, not single outcome) |
| **Objective collapse** (GRW, CSL, Penrose–Diósi) | a **real physical process with a threshold** | **yes — testable** |

Only the last row makes predictions distinct from textbook QM, and it is being tested experimentally. This table is the map; U-Theory must locate itself on it.

## 2. THE U-THEORY MAPPING

> **QMC-1 (Triadic reading of measurement — L3).** A quantum superposition is an **uncommitted virtual triad**: Form, Position and Action are correlated (entangled) but not yet bound to definite substrate resources (Time, Space, Energy). Decoherence is the substrate **monitoring** the triad; measurement/collapse is the triad **compiling** into a definite, substrate-committed state — a *stable mirror projection* (`THE_MIRROR_THEORY`), i.e. an observable particle/outcome.

The honest anchor is **decoherence theory** (L1, Zurek): an open system entangles with its environment, which *einselects* a preferred **pointer basis** and suppresses interference between pointer states. In triadic language:
- **superposition** = F/P/A correlated, substrate-uncommitted;
- **environment monitoring** = the substrate (T/S/E) "reading" the triad;
- **einselection of pointer states** = which Forms/Positions/Actions are *substrate-compatible* (survive compilation);
- **the QTC result** (`APPENDIX_QTC` §8) is the same physics seen from the codec side: an exposed superposition is destroyed by the environment (compiled), a symmetry-protected one is not (it pays no substrate tax for that channel).

This mapping is **coherent and L1-anchored as far as einselection goes.** But einselection is not the whole story (§6).

## 5. THE OBJECTIVE BRANCH — WHERE IT COULD BECOME PHYSICS

> **QMC-3 (Resource-threshold collapse — L2, hypothesis).** If a virtual triad collapses when its substrate-uncommitted cost density exceeds a critical value $\rho^{\text{crit}}$, then U-Theory collapse is an **objective-collapse model**, structurally analogous to Penrose Orchestrated Reduction.

The analogy is exact in form. **Penrose–Diósi OR:** a superposition of two mass distributions self-collapses in a time
$$\tau \sim \frac{\hbar}{E_G},$$
where $E_G$ is the gravitational self-energy of the *difference* of the two mass distributions — i.e. collapse triggers when the superposition "costs" too much gravitational energy. **GRW/CSL** instead posit a spontaneous-localization rate $\lambda$ and length $r_C$.

The U-Theory move is to replace "gravitational self-energy" with the MMT **existence-tax / meaning-cost** of holding the triad uncommitted: collapse when the accumulated cost of *not* compiling crosses $\rho^{\text{crit}}$. This is the **only** way "collapse = crystallized meaning" becomes a claim about the world rather than about our bookkeeping.

**What it then owes (and this is the whole game):**
1. **Specify the threshold** $\rho^{\text{crit}}$ in measurable units (mass? information? action?), with a formula playing the role of Penrose's $E_G$.
2. **Predict the deviation** from unitary QM (a collapse rate; a spontaneous heating; a coherence-time ceiling for large superpositions).
3. **Survive the bounds.** Objective-collapse models are already constrained — by macroscopic-superposition interferometry, ultracold mechanical oscillators, underground spontaneous-X-ray-emission searches, and space-based force-noise (LISA-Pathfinder-class) limits. Any U-Theory threshold must fit inside the *surviving* region (and most of GRW/CSL parameter space is already excluded or pressured).

If U-Theory **cannot or will not** produce step 1, it has chosen branch (E): it is interpretation, full stop — and should say so. If it **can**, it joins a real, falsifiable research program (and risks being falsified, which is the price of being physics).

## 5.1 "Mass = magnitude of Form" — the lever, and why it is already constrained

The intuition *mass is the magnitude of the Form* (Form↔Time) has a literal physical anchor: a massive particle carries an internal clock at the **Compton frequency** $\omega_C = mc^2/\hbar$ (de Broglie's internal clock; probed by Compton-clock interferometry). So "Form-magnitude" = the rate of the persistence-clock, and "bigger Form costs more Time" is the relativistic $E=\gamma mc^2$ co-scaling. Inertia (Position's resistance ∝ mass) and $E=mc^2$ (Action-magnitude ∝ mass) complete the triadic gloss. **As a re-reading of special relativity this is coherent but predicts nothing new** (the §4 (E) branch). It becomes physics only when Form-magnitude *sets the collapse rate*:

> **QMC-3a (Mass-dependent collapse threshold — L2, falsifiable).** If Form-magnitude $\Phi$ sets the rate at which an uncommitted triad compiles,
> $$\tau_{\text{collapse}} \;\sim\; \frac{\hbar}{\kappa\,\Phi},$$
> then larger Forms crystallize sooner — structurally **Penrose–Diósi OR**.

**Which quantity is $\Phi$ decides everything** (the choice the corpus must make):

| $\Phi$ = Form-magnitude | Reduces to | Experimental status |
|---|---|---|
| (i) rest energy $mc^2$ / Compton freq $mc^2/\hbar$ | bare mass-energy | too coarse — OR depends on the *difference* of mass distributions, not total mass |
| (ii) **gravitational self-energy of the mass-difference $E_G$** | **Penrose–Diósi**, $\tau\sim\hbar/E_G$ (e.g. $E_G\sim Gm^2/R$) | **the parameter-free version is EXCLUDED** (Donadi et al., *Nat. Phys.* 2021, underground spontaneous-X-ray test); survives only with a regularization length $R_0$ (a free parameter, bounded from below) |
| (iii) information content / number of distinguished alternatives | GRW/CSL-type "amount of superposition" | constrained by CSL bounds ($\lambda, r_C$); large regions excluded/pressured |

The most natural U-Theory reading — *a bigger, more-separated Form costs more to hold uncommitted* — lands on **(ii)**, gravitational self-energy. That is the honest payoff and the honest cost:

> **Verdict (QMC-3a).** "Mass = magnitude of Form" gives the collapse hypothesis **exactly one testable face** (mass-dependent, OR-form) — and that face is **already under severe experimental pressure, with its simplest (parameter-free) version already falsified.** U-Theory therefore inherits genuine falsifiability and must either **(a)** *derive* the regularization length $R_0$ from the MMT existence-tax first-principles (hard, and the only route to new physics), or **(b)** accept that collapse is the epistemic (E) branch — interpretation. **No free lunch here either:** the idea is testable precisely because it can be wrong, and the easy version is.

This is the strongest honest statement available: the relativity-gloss (mass = Form-magnitude, $E=\gamma mc^2$) is coherent re-description; its *one* route to physics is a mass-dependent objective-collapse threshold that is already being measured — and which, in its simplest form, has already failed.

## 5.2 The shape of the door — why the threshold has no novel, surviving form

A serious attempt to specify $\rho^{\text{crit}}$ does not open a door; it reveals the door's *shape*. For "existence-tax" to play Penrose's $E_G$ it must become a physical quantity with units. There are exactly **three** candidates, and each terminates:

1. **Mass / gravitational self-energy.** Then $\tau\sim\hbar/E_G$ *is* Penrose–Diósi, and coupling to mass density *is* CSL. Real physics — but a **rediscovery under a new name**: zero novelty, zero "meaning."
2. **Information / configurational distinguishability** (collapse rate grows with how distinguishable the branches are). The **only** potentially-new path — it would predict collapse for superpositions that are informationally distinct but gravitationally similar, which mass-based models do not. But it is **strongly constrained**: a mass-independent, distinguishability-coupled collapse law is not *generically* falsified until its rate is specified — yet **any rate large enough to explain ordinary measurement collapse while remaining mass-independent is strongly constrained** by long-baseline photon, atom, molecule, and matter-wave interference experiments (e.g. superpositions of >25 kDa molecules), which preserve coherence exactly where such a law predicts collapse. **This is precisely why every working collapse model (GRW/CSL/DP) couples to mass, not information.** To survive the data the coupling would have to be effectively mass-weighted — collapsing case 2 back into case 1.
3. **Semantic meaning.** No units, no conserved quantity, no rate. **Dead at dimensional analysis.** (This is §9's retraction, seen from the physics side.)

> **Structural no-go map (QMC-3b) — not a formal impossibility theorem.** The move that makes the threshold *novel* (information/distinguishability coupling) is the move existing quantum optics *already strongly constrains* (any rate large enough to matter is ruled out by matter-wave interferometry); the move that *survives* (mass coupling) erases the novelty and the "meaning"; semantics does not survive dimensional analysis. Hence §5 has **no novel, surviving form**: the objective branch either **reduces to Penrose–Diósi/CSL** (real but not new) or is **excluded** (new but wrong). This is not a door we failed to open — it is the actual geometry of the option space, documented.

This delimitation **is** the scientific output of the §5 inquiry: a clean **no-go map**, which is a legitimate, publishable negative result. (The structural argument does not depend on precise numbers; the *current* CSL/DP exclusion bounds should be checked against the latest literature, as the field moves.)

## 5.3 The decision test — does MMT supply a collapse scale? (Dimensional analysis: no.)

The bounded version of path (a): rather than hunt, *check* whether `APPENDIX_MMT`'s existence-tax formalism contains an **independent length or energy scale** that could fix the collapse parameter $R_0$ in the surviving window (sub-Ångström to atomic, $\sim10^{-10}\,$m). The answer is decidable by dimensional analysis of MMT's own equations.

- $\mathcal{M} = \int_0^T U\,d\tau$ with $U=\sqrt[3]{F\cdot P\cdot A}$ **dimensionless** (a score in $[0,1]$); the conservation law $\mathcal{M}+\text{Stupidity}=T$ forces $\mathcal{M}$ to have units of **time** (s).
- MMC-1, $m c^2 \sim \kappa\,\mathcal{M}$, then forces $\kappa$ to have units of **power** (J/s = W).
- $\kappa$ is the **only** dimensionful constant MMT introduces — and the corpus **explicitly leaves it free/uncalibrated** (MMT §4.1: "we do not currently know its numerical value"; §11–§12: "calibrating $\kappa$ is an open task").

> **Boundary theorem (QMC-3c, robust form).** The framework contains **no fundamental dimensionful constant** — no length, no energy, no speed of its own. Its only fixed number, $\varphi\approx0.618$, is **dimensionless** and sets no scale. The corrected units of $\mathcal{M}$ (seconds) do **not** help: those seconds are *accumulated solvent-time* — a system- and observation-dependent **variable**, not a constant of nature (unlike $\hbar/E$ at fixed $E$, or the Planck time). The single dimensionful constant linking meaning to physics is $\kappa$ (power, from $mc^2\sim\kappa\mathcal{M}$), and it is **explicitly free/uncalibrated**. So the route to any collapse rate, $1/\tau\sim\kappa\,\Phi/\hbar$, inherits two holes: $\kappa$ free, and $\Phi$ (the "meaning-cost" of a superposition) with no operational quantum definition. **A free parameter is moved into $\kappa$, never derived.** New physics requires a fundamental constant the framework does not contain; importing $\hbar,c,G$ to build one merely reconstructs Penrose–Diósi/CSL.
>
> **Resolution-independence.** This does not turn on whether $\mathcal{M}$ is called *dimensionless* or *time-valued*. If dimensionless (via a reference time $\tau_0$, $\mathcal{M}=\tau_0^{-1}\!\int U\,d\tau$), then $\tau_0$ is itself a free scale; if time-valued, $\kappa$ is the free constant. **Either reading yields a *free* scale; neither yields a *fundamental* one.** *(Note an internal inconsistency to reconcile: `APPENDIX_DIM` calls meaning "dimensionless" while MPI-1/MMC-1 imply seconds. The corpus currently asserts both — a reviewer will catch it — but the result here is independent of which is adopted.)*
>
> **Ledger fix (QMC-IL.1).** To end the ambiguity cleanly, carry *two* symbols: $\mu \equiv \tau_0^{-1}\!\int U\,d\tau$ (dimensionless normalised meaning — DIM's usage) and $\mathcal{M}_T \equiv \int U\,d\tau$ (time-valued stability integral — MMT's $mc^2\sim\kappa\,\mathcal{M}_T$). Neither introduces a *fundamental* scale; $\tau_0$ and $\kappa$ remain free.
>
> **Triadicity is in the recipe, not the dimension.** $U=(F\cdot P\cdot A)^{1/3}$ is the geometric mean of three dimensionless sub-scores — itself a dimensionless number. The "three" lives in *how $U$ is built*, not in its units and not in any constant; it therefore does not touch the collapse-rate question at all.

**Decision (this resolves §5).** MMT supplies no independent collapse scale ⇒ the objective (O) branch has **no first-principles realisation** in U-Theory ⇒ collapse defaults to the **epistemic (E) branch — interpretation**, shown by dimensional analysis rather than asserted. This is consistent with the wider fact that *no* objective-collapse model (CSL's $\lambda$, DP's $R_0$) has ever derived its constant from first principles — the open wound of the whole program; MMT's structure shows it cannot close it either.

This negative result *is* the deliverable: a precise statement of **what MMT cannot claim** about quantum collapse — a falsifiability boundary for the theory, publishable as such.

## 6. WHAT DECOHERENCE DOES AND DOES NOT EXPLAIN (no overclaim)

Honesty about the L1 anchor:
- **Decoherence explains:** the pointer basis (which Forms/Positions/Actions are stable), the suppression of interference, the *emergence of classicality*, and *why ordinary macroscopic superpositions become environmentally unobservable extremely fast under normal conditions* (it does **not** forbid engineered, isolated mesoscopic/macroscopic superpositions). This is solid and it is genuinely the physical content behind "the substrate compiles the triad."
- **Decoherence does NOT explain:** why a **single** outcome is realized (the "and→or" transition), nor does it *derive* the Born rule from nothing (derivations exist but assume extra principles — envariance, Gleason-type constraints). That residue is exactly the measurement problem, and it is where §4's fork lives.

So "compilation = decoherence" is **half true**: decoherence is the compilation's *mechanism of basis selection*, but the *commitment to one branch* is the part that is either epistemic (E) or a new physical process (O).

## 7. CONNECTION TO THE MEASURED QTC RESULT

> **Scope of this section (red note):** QTC validates the substrate-compilation metaphor **only at the decoherence / noise-symmetry level** — it is *not* evidence for objective collapse and does not bear on the E/O fork.

`APPENDIX_QTC` is the experimental shadow of this picture. There, a logical superposition recorded in a symmetry-protected (shared-Form) subspace **resisted compilation** by the environment (fidelity 1.0 in the statevector *simulation* under collective dephasing), while an exposed one was **compiled away / decohered operationally** (it lost interference through the measured decoherence channel) as $\cos^2\theta$. *Precision (per the hardware):* on IBM `ibm_marrakesh` (`APPENDIX_QTC` §8.3) the boundary was confirmed **two-sidedly** — $R\approx+0.97$ under **injected** collective noise, but $R\approx0$ under the device's **native (independent)** noise. So the "fidelity 1.0" is the idealized simulation; the chip showed compilation is **noise-symmetry-gated**, not automatic. In QMC language: the protected triad pays **no substrate tax** for that noise channel, so the substrate cannot compile it; the exposed triad does, so it crystallizes. This is consistent, real, and L1 — but note it is ordinary decoherence, and says nothing about the (E)/(O) fork.
