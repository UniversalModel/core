## 0. ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY

U-Theory says: **existence is a continuous transaction**. To exist, any system must keep paying three (or more) currencies simultaneously: **Form** (T-tax), **Position** (S-tax), **Action** (E-tax). The integral of successful payments over time is **meaning** (ℳ = ∫U dτ). Meaning has no dimension, but the **amount of meaning required to remain stable** scales with the number of currencies the substrate can hold. Add a currency → unlock a dimension. Remove a currency → collapse a dimension. Civilizations, cells, qubits, and stars are all special cases of the same accounting equation; they differ only in how many currencies they pay and how recursively they decompose their payments.
