## 0. THE ONE TEST (what "prove" means here)

> **A claim is scientifically provable only if its negation would look different in an experiment.** If "the substrate is meaning" and "the substrate is quantum information/amplitude" produce *identical* experiments, no experiment can decide between them — the claim is then a choice of ontology, not a theorem. The whole map below is organized around finding **one distinct prediction**.

## 1. THE THESIS, DECOMPOSED INTO THREE LAYERS

"The world arises from proto-superposition (= primary meaning) that crystallizes into matter" is **not one claim** — it is three, with three different epistemic statuses:

| Layer | Claim | Status |
|-------|-------|--------|

Matter *is* excitations of quantum fields; particles emerge from the quantum vacuum; superposition is fundamental; nonlocality (Bell) is real and local-hidden-variable models are excluded. This is mainstream (QFT; Wheeler's "it from bit"). **U-Theory does not need to prove this — it needs to map onto it**, which it does in F/P/A vocabulary. Claim this as a coherent *re-framing* of established physics; it is defensible and strong **as framing**, and requires no new evidence.

## 3. LAYER 2 — THE MEANING-IDENTIFICATION (unprovable by construction)

Calling the substrate "meaning" is an ontological interpretation in the **it-from-bit / QBism** lineage. Such positions are respected — and *never proven*, because they make no distinct prediction. The triadic version provides two beautiful, **physically-anchored** sub-readings — and both are re-descriptions, not predictions:

### 3.1 Form ↔ Time: *mass = magnitude of Form*
Anchor: a particle's internal **Compton clock** $\omega_C=mc^2/\hbar$ — mass as the rate of the persistence-clock; $E=\gamma mc^2$ as the Time-price (see `APPENDIX_QMC` §5.1). Real anchor; but as stated it re-describes special relativity — **no new prediction**.

### 3.2 Position ↔ Space: *nonlocality = unripe Position; localization pays the space tax*
**Anchor (real, and elegant):** the **energy cost of localization**. Heisenberg $\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \hbar/2$ ⇒ confining a particle to a region $\Delta x$ costs kinetic energy $\sim \hbar^2/(2m\,\Delta x^2)$ (zero-point / particle-in-a-box). Smaller Space ⇒ higher Position-price. An entangled/nonlocal system has **no definite individual location** — an *unripe* Position that has *not yet paid*; measurement *ripens* it (localizes), and the price is paid. This maps cleanly onto Position↔Space ($B_S$) and complements `APPENDIX_DP` §DP-S6 ("entanglement = shared Form"): the pair shares one Form **and** carries unripe Positions.
**Anchor caveat (a physicist will catch this):** confinement energy $\hbar^2/(2m\,\Delta x^2)$ is the cost to *localize a single particle* — it does **not** anchor *nonlocality*. Entanglement is **correlation between systems**, not spatial extension of one wavefunction: two polarization-entangled photons can be perfectly localized in space yet remain maximally Bell-nonlocal. So this mapping **conflates single-particle delocalization with entanglement** — different phenomena. Honest residue: "localizing a particle costs energy" is real, but it is **not** a reading of nonlocality.
**Honest status:** even setting the conflation aside, as interpretation it reproduces standard QM exactly — no distinct prediction.

### 3.3 Action ↔ Energy: *chaos = unripe Action; ripens by paying energy*
**Anchor (real):** the thermodynamic cost of order. Indeterminate dynamics — "chaos," many possible outcomes — becoming a definite, committed action requires energy: **Landauer's principle** (resolving/erasing indeterminacy costs $\ge k_B T\ln 2$ per bit, experimentally confirmed — Bérut et al. 2012) and the **2nd law / negentropy** (local order requires energy input and entropy export — Schrödinger; Prigogine dissipative structures, already cited in `APPENDIX_MMT`). So "unripe Action (chaos) ripens (becomes definite / ordered) by paying with Energy" ↔ the energy price of negentropy and of committing the indeterminate. Action↔Energy ($B_E$), exactly.
**Anchor caveat:** Landauer's bound is for *erasure* (a logically irreversible operation), **not** for *an outcome becoming definite*. A measurement that creates a record is not erasure — in the unitary view nothing is erased. Invoking Landauer for "definiteness costs energy" **borrows its authority for a claim it does not make**; that claim (an energy cost of collapse/definiteness) is exactly the *contested* objective-collapse point, not its resolution.
**Honest status:** as interpretation it re-describes thermodynamics + Landauer — no distinct prediction beyond them, and the erasure↔definiteness link is not established.

> **The triad is now complete and symmetric** — each channel has an "unripe → ripe, pays its currency" reading with a *real* anchor: Form (uncommitted identity / pays Time / Compton clock), Position (nonlocality / pays Space / confinement energy), Action (chaos / pays Energy / Landauer–negentropy). The general superposition is *all three unripe at once* — the uncommitted virtual triad. Elegant; but symmetry is not prediction (next section).

> **Layer-2 verdict.** Each sub-reading has a *genuine physical anchor* (Compton clock; confinement energy; measurement). But an anchor is not a prediction. By the §0 test, Layer 2 is **interpretation** — honored (good company), not proven.

## 4. LAYER 3 — THE ONLY ROUTE TO PROOF (and why both anchors hit the same wall)

To leave interpretation, Layer 2 must yield **one observable that standard QM does not predict**. Strikingly, *both* triadic anchors, when operationalized, land on the **same frontier**:

- **Mass = Form** → "bigger Form crystallizes sooner" → a mass-dependent objective collapse → **Penrose–Diósi OR** ($\tau\sim\hbar/E_G$).
- **Nonlocality = unripe Position paying a space/energy cost** → "localization carries a real energy cost beyond standard measurement" → this is precisely **CSL's spontaneous localization with spontaneous heating** (a tiny energy non-conservation accompanying localization).
- **Chaos = unripe Action paying energy to ripen** → "an indeterminate outcome becoming definite carries an energy cost" → again **CSL-type spontaneous heating** (classically: the Landauer / negentropy cost of resolving indeterminacy). And since **environmental noise is a form of chaos** — the uncommitted Action of the surroundings — the decoherence that *compiles* a superposition is the environment's chaos forcing the system's triad to commit; **the QTC-measured noise is exactly this compiling agent.** *(Honest caveat: in physics "noise" is stochastic and "chaos" is deterministic-but-unpredictable — they coincide only at the ontological level of indeterminate Action, not as the same dynamical object.)*

So the meaning-thesis, made testable through *either* channel, becomes an **objective-collapse / spontaneous-localization model** — and that frontier is **already constrained and partly excluded**: Donadi et al. 2021 (rules out parameter-free Diósi–Penrose); CSL bounds from matter-wave interferometry (>25 kDa molecules), LISA Pathfinder rotational noise (2025), underground radiation searches. And `APPENDIX_QMC` §5.3 shows U-Theory's own formalism contains **no fundamental dimensionful constant** to *derive* such a threshold — it can only postulate one.

> **The convergence carries ZERO confirmatory weight — it is the geometry of the problem, not evidence.** All three channels terminate at objective collapse for one reason: **objective collapse is the only route by which a measurement *interpretation* becomes a *new-physics claim* — not the only possible measurement ontology.** Many-Worlds, Bohmian mechanics, and QBist/relational readings are legitimate measurement ontologies that make **no** new prediction (they are empirically equivalent to textbook QM); only objective collapse (CSL/DP/Penrose) predicts a measurable deviation. A theory of measurement becomes *physics* only by predicting such a deviation. So three triadic intuitions landing there is **not** a sign the triad is special — it is a sign **the door to new physics is one**. It does not support the framework; it merely locates where the (still-missing) prediction would have to live.

## 5. THE PROOF CHECKLIST (what a future derivation must supply)

To convert "the world is crystallized meaning" from interpretation to physics, a derivation must deliver **all** of:
1. **An operational definition** of the "meaning-gradient" / Position-ripening cost in measurable units — not borrowed from $\hbar,c,G$ (which just rebuilds OR/CSL).
2. **A fundamental scale** the framework *contains* (φ is dimensionless; κ is a free power; ℳ's seconds are a variable — none qualify; see QMC §5.3).
3. **A distinct prediction**: a collapse/localization rate, spontaneous heating, force noise, or coherence-time ceiling that differs from standard QM.
4. **Survival** of current CSL/DP bounds.
Miss any one → the thesis remains a (legitimate) interpretation.

## 6. VERDICT

- **Layer 1** (world from quantum substrate; superposition/nonlocality primary): **established** — claim it as framing, no proof needed.
- **Layer 2** (substrate *is* meaning; nonlocality *is* unripe Position): **interpretation by construction** — beautiful, anchored, in it-from-bit company, but not provable, because it makes no distinct prediction. This is a property of the claim, not a failure of effort.
- **Layer 3** (crystallization as new physics): **open but blocked** — the one route to proof runs through an objective-localization prediction the framework cannot currently derive and that experiment already pressures.

> **The honest path to the goal:** stop trying to "prove the bundle." Take Layer 1 as won; keep Layer 2 as the strongest interpretation in good company; and aim all energy at the single, precise Layer-3 target — *one derived, surviving, distinct prediction*. Find it and the thesis becomes physics; until then it is a coherent picture of the world, honestly labeled. That label is not a defeat — it is what keeps the door to "someday someone proves it" actually open.
