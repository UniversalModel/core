Why line 1 carries a moral clause

Judged for descriptive accuracy alone, Jev preferred "Accepting yourself and living by your own values" (0.89). The author added a moral requirement on purpose: a definition of happiness that claims universal recognition must not advertise happiness for people who harm others. Under that requirement Jev placed respect for the freedom and dignity of others first, in line with Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. It gave the plain prohibition "without harming others" 0.03, because harm is an ambiguous word.
