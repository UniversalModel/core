**TE IS NOT (the four non-negotiables):**

3. **NOT autonomous and NOT closed-loop.** TE proposes orderings and weak-pillar localizations to a named accountable human. It actuates nothing — no experiment, no synthesis, no clinical action, no policy, no market. Human-on-top is structural. **But "proposes orderings" is itself a control surface: deciding which of a specialist's open questions is the bottleneck and should be worked next is governance of the research agenda, not passive filing (§9).**

## 1. Thesis — the triadic engineer as dispatcher, not orchestra

**Triadic Engineering is the meta-discipline that orchestrates the specialized sciences by decomposing each onto the same three-bag partition (Form / Position / Action), scoring every node with the same non-compensatory keystone `U = ∛(F·P·A)`, and running them all on one shared multi-agent runtime so that structure learned in one domain can be transferred to another.** That is the whole claim. Everything grand the vision wants to say is downstream of that one sentence, and is **earned only if TE-P1/P2/P3 pass.** Until then the sentence above is the only thing on the table, and even it is **B3-pending.**

### 1.1 The cynical lead — sorting organizes access *and sets the queue*; it does not sew the clothes

Read the epigraph literally, because the literal reading is the honest one. **Sorting laundry does not sew a single stitch.** It does not weave new cloth or invent a new fabric. What it does is two things, and the second is the one humility likes to forget: it *organizes access* (turns an undivided pile into three labeled bags you can reach into in parallel, and makes the **empty bag** legible — the weakest-pillar signal: *here is why you have nothing to wear*), **and it sets the order in which the bags get worked.** This maps onto the discipline with no slack:

- **The clothes** = the ground truth. A DFT formation energy. A measured rate constant. A theorem with a proof. An atlas of cell types. A clinical outcome. **TE produces none of these.** It has no laboratory, no measurement apparatus, no proof procedure of its own.
- **The washing machines** = the specialized sciences. Each already produces its own clean ground truth by its own validated method. TE does not improve the wash cycle and does not replace the machine.
- **The sorting** = the F/P/A decomposition + the `U`-score + the scheduler + β-transfer. This is the *only* thing TE adds. It is real work — but it is **filing-and-dispatch, not science.** And "dispatch" is the honest half: deciding *which weak pillar in which domain is worked next* is agenda-setting, a governance surface, not a passive index (§9).

The discipline's actual contribution is *not new clothes — a closet with drawers and a priority queue.* Cheaper access, parallel search, an explicit "weakest pillar" diagnostic, and a principled channel for carrying a trick from one field to a structurally-isomorphic problem in another. Genuinely useful. Not a new science, and saying it is would be a lie the author refuses to tell — and not "mere filing" either, because that would hide the queue.

### 1.2 Dispatcher, not orchestra (the controlling metaphor, with its distortion named)

The same truth, in the register the vision reaches for: **the triadic engineer is the conductor/dispatcher, not the orchestra.** The orchestra is the specialists — the violinist (the chemist's DFT), the cellist (the biologist's assay), the brass (the physicist's measurement) — each producing the actual sound, the ground truth. The conductor produces no sound; stand him in an empty hall and you hear silence. **But be precise about what a conductor actually does, because the metaphor distorts gently downward if you aren't:** he does not merely "amplify." He sets tempo, decides which section leads, and *cues who plays next.* Transposed to TE, that cue is **cross-domain dispatch**: the scheduler reads the weakest-pillar field and *routes the next unit of compute/experiment to a specific specialist domain* (§2.4). A layer that decides which specialist works next, on which sub-problem, in what order, is a **manager with a priority queue** — not a silent bystander. The humble nouns ("filing," "access," "drawers") are milder than this mechanism, and that mildness is itself a misrepresentation to be guarded against, no less than the grandiose direction. A conductor who believes he can fire the orchestra and hum the symphony is deluded; a dispatcher who pretends he never touches the queue is dishonest. TE refuses both.

### 1.3 The honest floor

If the three falsifiers pass, the vision is the prize: an integrator who genuinely amplifies every science he conducts. If any one fails, the corresponding grand claim is **deleted, not parked** (symmetric retirement), and TE reverts — without embarrassment, because this was the deal from the first line — to what it always provably was: **an elegant index with a priority queue.** Useful. Worth building. Not a new science, and not "the greatest scientist." A failed falsifier may also produce a clean **negative result** — the discovery that some science's socks are sewn to its shirts, that its three bags leak. **We report that result; we do not market it.** A negative is a fact, neither a defeat to hide nor a virtue to collect (see §B.4 on why "the negative result is the real prize" framing is forbidden: a framework that books a win on every branch has stopped being falsifiable).

## 2. The mechanism — how the meta-discipline composes the existing parts

> **READ FIRST.** §2 wires together only inherited primitives (the triad + score from SSS; the `TriadicDomain` contract + scheduler + TAA jury from GSI-RTD; the instances TRB/TRC/TSE; the `3N+1` grid + GSM-Priority from TMAC; β from GSI-RTD §26.4). Its single contribution is a **composition convention**. It is **B1 where it is just wiring** and **B3-pending where it claims to beat a baseline.** No sentence here is a finding.

### 2.1 The pipeline at a glance — five stages, four of them already built

```
   ① UNIVERSAL TRIAD            F / P / A           (SSS / UCT) ───────────────┐  given
        │  the minimum complete partition; U=∛(F·P·A) non-compensatory         │
        ▼                                                                       │
   ② PER-DOMAIN DECOMPOSITION   each science ⟶ a TriadicDomain                 │  per-domain
        │  TRB(biology.organism) · TRC(chemistry.substance) · TSE(clinic) · …   │  P0 gates
        │  EACH gated by its OWN separability falsifier (the per-domain P0)     │  (TE-P1)
        ▼                                                                       │
   ③ TRIADIC-ELEMENT DATABASES  F/P/A-typed, U-scored, provenance-tagged rows ──┤  storage
        │  one schema across domains; domains CONTRIBUTE, never overwrite       │
        ▼                                                                       │
   ④ MULTI-AGENT ORCHESTRATION  GSI-RTD scheduler + TMAC grid                  │  DISPATCH
        │  weakest-pillar routing, now ACROSS domains (the priority queue)      │  (TE-P3)
        ▼                                                                       │
   ⑤ β CROSS-DOMAIN TRANSFER    reuse STRUCTURE across drawers ─────────────────┘  transfer
           β = structural_similarity(D_source, D_target)  (GSI-RTD §26.4)          (TE-P2)
```

The only genuinely new objects are **③** (the record format) and the **cross-domain** scope of **④** and **⑤**. Stages ① and ② exist verbatim in SSS/UCT and in TRB/TRC/TSE. Canonical execution order: **decompose each science (②, gate with its P0) → emit typed records (③) → dispatch across the corpus (④) → transfer structure where β warrants it (⑤).**

### 2.2 Stage ② — every science becomes a `TriadicDomain`, gated by its own P0

A science enters the meta-layer **only** if it passes its own per-domain separability falsifier (its P0) — **this is TE-P1.** If a domain's F/P/A do not factor (TRC's DFT-from-one-structure confound; TRB's atlas collinearity), its `embed_form / build_position_graph / enumerate_actions` return three transforms of *one* number, the GSM off-diagonal `ρ_FPA → 1`, and the U-posterior degenerates to a near-point with **no triadic structure.** Such a domain gets not a clean interface but a **scalarizer wearing three hats**, and its records cannot be cross-linked without leaking. **The per-domain P0 is the doorman.** A domain that fails it is **left out** until it passes, and the "all sciences" claim shrinks by exactly one science (symmetric retirement). This is the first place the vision can die quietly: if most sciences turn out non-separable, "decompose all sciences" reduces to "decompose the *few* that happen to factor," and the meta-discipline is a niche convention. **TE works only over the separable sub-corpus, and that sub-corpus's size is an empirical unknown, not an assumption.**

### 2.3 Stage ③ — the triadic-element database: what a "triadic element" actually is

The vision's "huge databases of triadic elements" is, demystified, **one row type** — an F/P/A-typed, U-scored, provenance-tagged record:

| Field | Type | Source primitive | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `node_id` | string | domain-local | the one declared node this row scores (a substance, an organ, a disease, a policy) |
| `domain` | enum `{biology, chemistry, clinic, …}` | which `TriadicDomain` emitted it | the drawer label |
| `F` | embedding `float[d_F]` | `embed_form` | identity/structure score on `[0,1]` + its embedding |
| `P` | graph node `(id, edges, meta)` | `build_position_graph` | context/position score + local topology |
| `A` | action primitive set | `enumerate_actions` | reactivity/function score + the action repertoire |
| `U, δ, SI` | floats | SSS keystone | `U=∛(F·P·A)`, `δ=(max−min)/(max+0.01)`, `SI=U/(1+δ)²` |
| `π*` | enum `{F,P,A}` | `argmin` leaf (TMAC §11.4) | the **weakest pillar** — the routing address |
| `provenance` | record | GSM `(μ,τ)` + claim envelope | who computed it, how, with what coverage/confidence, **B-level** |
| `separability_status` | enum | the domain's P0 result | `{separable, axis_unseparated, P0_pending}` — gates cross-linking |

**The load-bearing discipline is in the last two fields, not the first six.** A triadic element is **not** a fact; it is a *claim with a confidence interval, a method trail, and a B-level stamp.* A row whose `provenance` says `axis_unseparated` renders **"not yet interpretable — separability/measurement pending"** and is **inadmissible to cross-domain linking** until its P0 clears. Each `TriadicDomain` is the **sole writer** of its own rows at the *storage* layer; contribution is **append-and-stamp, never overwrite.**
> **Scope of the "sole writer" guarantee (read with §2.5).** This guarantee is about *storage* — no domain overwrites another's rows. It is **NOT** a guarantee of agenda autonomy: β-transfer (§2.5) biases the *routing prior* of a target domain *before its specialist acts*, so a heuristic learned elsewhere can steer which of the target domain's questions gets worked next. The specialist remains sole *author of their rows*; they are **not** sole author of their *work queue* once transfer is on. The storage guarantee must not be read as a promise of specialist autonomy in general — §2.5 narrows it on purpose.

**The database is an index over specialist output, not a replacement for it** — the drawer holds the clothes the specialist sewed; it does not sew. The **O5b firewall holds on the storage layer**: a query asking to "rank these nodes by Δℳ/cost" across rows is **type-forbidden at the schema level**, not merely discouraged. The database can tell you *which pillar of one node is weak*; it cannot tell you *which node is worthier.* That operation does not exist. (The intra-node value-frame leak of §A.6(3) is *not* closed by this schema rule — it lives inside a single node's declared objective, which the schema permits.)

### 2.4 Stage ④ — dispatch: the same scheduler, now routing across drawers

The **GSI-RTD scheduler** (§20: hard gates G1–G4 — including the load-bearing **G4** `min(F,P,A)≈0` weakest-pillar veto — then the geometric `Score = ∛(E[SI]·E[ROI]·(1−Risk))·(1−Redundancy)^γ`) and the **TMAC** grid (`4·3+4+1` agents per node, GSM-Priority routing) are lifted **verbatim.** The single change is the **scope of the candidate set**: in TE the scheduler ranks triadic elements **across the corpus**, and the weakest-pillar signal becomes a **cross-domain dispatcher.**

```
weakest pillar π* of a node  ⟶  the PILLAR-AGENT that owns it  ⟶  the DOMAIN whose specialist must act next
        (gate G4: min(F,P,A)≈0 = veto)            (TMAC §11.4)            (the dispatcher's cue)
GSM-Priority(node) = E[ΔP_fail | intervene on π*] / ∛(C_time·C_space·C_energy)     (one node, never cross-node)
```

This is the precise sense in which the triadic engineer is a **dispatcher, not the orchestra.** The scheduler never does the biology or the chemistry; it reads the `π*` field of the highest-leverage element and **routes the next unit of compute/experiment to the agent — and therefore the specialist domain — that owns the bottleneck pillar.** A weak *Position* pillar on a clinical node routes to a context/perfusion question; a weak *Action* pillar on a materials node routes to a reactivity calculation. Non-compensation is what makes this work: because `U→0` when any pillar →0, the gradient of the aggregate is largest at the smallest pillar, so "which drawer is empty" is computed automatically. A weighted sum gives a *ranking*; `GSM-Priority` gives a *responsible sub-problem and its home domain* — the routing affordance is the genuine, and **only**, mechanical novelty of orchestration. **This routing is agenda-setting over specialists' open questions — a governance surface, not a neutral index (§1.2, §9).** **TE-P3** is the claim that this dispatch earns its keep against a monolithic baseline at equal budget.

### 2.5 Stage ⑤ — β cross-domain transfer: reuse *structure*, not surface labels — and the construct-validity hole, named

The interdisciplinary promise is operationalized by **exactly one** inherited mechanism — the GSI-RTD §26.4 transfer coefficient. There is no separate "interweaving engine."

```
impact_target(f,p,a) = β · impact_source(f,p,a) + (1−β) · prior(f,p,a)
β = structural_similarity(D_src, D_tgt)
  = ⅓·[ cosine(mean_F_embed(D_src), mean_F_embed(D_tgt))      # do the Forms embed alike?
      + subgraph_iso(G_P(D_src), G_P(D_tgt))                  # do the Position graphs share topology?
      + jaccard(action_set(D_src), action_set(D_tgt)) ]       # do the Action repertoires overlap?
```

Per **Prop. 26.1**, β rewards *isomorphic structure, not similar vocabulary*: two domains transfer to the degree their F-embeddings, P-topologies, and A-repertoires are isomorphic at some recursion depth *d*, never because they "sound related." A stability heuristic transfers from biology to materials only if the *shape* of the problem (a context graph whose collapse zeroes the aggregate) is the same shape — regardless of whether one says "tissue perfusion" and the other says "phase-field connectivity." The guardrails are inherited and **self-deleting**: **FM-7 negative transfer** → set **β=0**, flag the pair non-isomorphic; **FM-4 distribution shift** → clamp β to 0.1.

> **β CONSTRUCT-VALIDITY HOLE (stated, not papered over).** β is *always computable*, but the formula above averages three quantities on **incommensurable scales** — a cosine on learned embeddings ∈ [−1,1], a subgraph-isomorphism score (exact iso is NP-hard, so this is some **unspecified approximation** — a graph-edit-distance kernel or GNN surrogate — whose monotonicity is not established here), and a Jaccard ratio ∈ [0,1] — with an **unjustified equal ⅓ weighting**, and it presumes that a biology F-embedding and a chemistry F-embedding live in a space where their cosine *means* anything. None of those three assumptions is argued. **Consequence for the falsifier:** if β is a noisy composite, a TE-P2 null is *uninterpretable* — failed transfer and failed metric are confounded. Therefore, until TE-P2's construct-validity arm (§B.2) is passed, **β is labeled an EXPLORATORY HEURISTIC, not a method.** The word "method" is reserved for after β is shown to (a) be invariant to the embedding/iso-approximation choices within tolerance and (b) predict realized gain. Calling β a "coefficient" is shorthand, not a claim of validated measurement.

**TE-P2** is the claim that this beats a domain-specific baseline *and* that β (not surface similarity) is what carries the gain.

### 2.6 Worked sketch — a biology stability heuristic applied to a materials problem (ILLUSTRATIVE; B3-pending; zero evidence; fully abstract numbers)

> **THIS IS A CONSISTENCY ILLUSTRATION, NOT A RESULT.** No transfer was run, no data touched, no β estimated. **All numbers below are abstract placeholders (`U_low`, `π*=Position`) — no specific clinical or materials node's scored values are borrowed**, precisely so the illustration cannot smuggle false concreteness by reusing a real-looking figure. The numbers show the *pipeline shape* only. Every step is gated; the most valuable real outcome would be this sketch **failing** TE-P1/P2/P3. RUO — not a device, not a protocol.

**The source heuristic (biology, TRB).** Organ-scale failures repeatedly localize to a collapsed **Position** pillar: the tissue is structurally intact (F high) and functional (A high) but **cut off from its context** — its position graph has lost the edges that supply it (the perfusion-context-collapse pattern; abstractly `F high, A high, U = U_low (CRITICAL), π* = Position`). The learned heuristic, stated structurally: *when F and A are healthy but U is critically low, the lesion is an edge-deletion in the Position graph, and the corrective routes to re-establishing context connectivity, not to rebuilding the part.*

**The target problem (chemistry/materials, TRC).** A candidate solid-state material scores `F` high (thermodynamically reasonable, identity stable) and `A` high (desired reactivity in simulation), but `U` is CRITICAL with `π* = Position` — and in TRC the Position pillar carries `E_hull` and phase/connectivity context. The material's *intrinsic* chemistry is fine; what collapses it is its **place in the phase landscape** (off-hull, or a percolation gap in the microstructure).

**Why β is allowed to fire (the gate, not the hope).** The two domains are eligible only if `structural_similarity` is non-trivial *on the Position axis specifically*: the biology lesion is "an edge-deletion in a context graph that zeroes a non-compensatory aggregate," and the materials lesion is "an off-hull / connectivity gap in a context graph that zeroes the same aggregate." If `subgraph_iso(G_P^bio, G_P^mat)` is high — same *topology of context-collapse* — then β>0 on the structure that matters, **even though the vocabularies (perfusion vs. convex hull) share no words.** That is the whole content of "structural, not surface" — and it is only as trustworthy as β's construct validity (§2.5).

**What transfers, mechanically.** β > 0 means the biology-learned `impact_source(·)` biases the TRC scheduler's prior: it raises `E[ΔP_fail | intervene on Position]` for this class of node, so `GSM-Priority` routes the next compute to the **Position-agent** — to *re-establishing context connectivity* (stabilizing the phase/hull environment) rather than to the Action-agent (re-engineering reactivity) or the Form-agent (substituting identity). The transferred object is **the routing prior** — "when F,A are fine and Position is the floor, fix the context edges first" — **not any chemistry fact.** This is exactly the point at which the materials specialist is *no longer the sole author of their own work queue* (§2.3 caveat): a foreign domain's prior is steering the dispatch. The specialist (DFT, phase-diagram calculation, measured `E_hull`) still produces the ground truth that confirms or kills the routed hypothesis.

**The three ways this dies (each deletes a claim, not parks it).**

| If… | falsifier | what is removed |
|---|---|---|
| materials F/P/A don't separate (`E_hull` double-loads Position+Form; TRC-P0d) | **TE-P1** | the target domain is **ejected from the corpus**; no transfer is attempted |
| β-biased routing does **not** beat a TRC-native baseline on held-out tasks (CI ⊇ 0; FM-7 fires, β→0), OR β fails its construct-validity arm | **TE-P2** | the **transfer claim is retired** (β is metaphor, or β is an uninterpretable composite); the biology→materials "interweaving" is deleted |
| the routed stack doesn't beat a monolithic agent at equal budget | **TE-P3** | the **orchestration value is retired**; the engineer is a dispatcher with no demonstrated premium |

> **Net of the sketch.** The pipeline *can* express "a biology stability heuristic helped a materials problem" as a precise, falsifiable mechanism: a Position-axis routing prior transferred by structural isomorphism of context-collapse graphs, scored by the same `U=∛(F·P·A)`, gated by three pre-registered falsifiers. Whether it *does* anything is unknown and untested. The honest expectation is that it works in a **narrow, symmetry-gated regime** (the separable, structurally-isomorphic sub-corpus) and nowhere else — and that expectation, too, is a hypothesis to test, not a result to bank (§B.4).

> **Boxed law (§2, one line).** *The meta-discipline builds nothing new: it stamps each separable science's output as F/P/A-typed, U-scored, provenance-tagged triadic elements (③), lets the inherited weakest-pillar scheduler **dispatch** the next unit of work to the domain that owns the bottleneck pillar (④) — an agenda-setting act, not a neutral index — and reuses heuristics across domains only where β (an exploratory heuristic until validated) certifies structural — not surface — isomorphism (⑤); the engineer is the dispatcher, never the orchestra, and earns the name "scientist" only if TE-P1/P2/P3 all pass — otherwise it is an elegant index with a priority queue.*

## B. The three load-bearing, pre-registered falsifiers (symmetric retirement, with kill thresholds fixed before the data)

The vision is the prize **only if all three pass.** They are independent: each guards a distinct joint, and any one failing demotes the corresponding grand claim. They are pre-registered here — H0, metric, **decision threshold, and the explicit kill threshold** that triggers deletion are fixed **before** the data, so a null result cannot later be re-narrated as a partial win, and a failure cannot be reclassified as "the mechanism correctly down-weighting."

### B.1 — TE-P1: per-domain separability (does the laundry actually sort into three bags?)

**The Procrustean-bed guard.** The most seductive failure of a *universal* decomposition is that it becomes a Procrustean bed: every domain stretched or amputated until it "fits" three boxes. A universal claim that *cannot fail* to apply is **unfalsifiable, and therefore empty.** TE-P1 is the test that makes the universality claim able to fail — and the per-domain P0 doorman (§2.2) is the mechanism that *refuses entry* to a domain that doesn't factor, rather than forcing it into the boxes.

- **Claim under test:** each target science *factors* into separable F/P/A (the per-domain `P0`). If pillars are entangled — you cannot move Form without dragging Position — the "three bags" leak and `U=∛(F·P·A)` is built on coordinates that don't exist.
- **H0 (null):** the F/P/A assignment for domain *D* explains no more pillar-separable variance than a random three-way partition of the same feature set.
- **Metric:** conditional-independence statistics at the registry/class level (conditional dCor / CMI — **no Pearson |r|**; the 0.6–0.8 band is *inconclusive, never a pass*), plus a held-out perturbation test (perturbing one pillar's inputs moves only that pillar's score; cross-pillar leakage `< τ_sep`, default `τ_sep = 0.15`).
- **PASS threshold:** measured F/P/A partition beats the best random three-way partition by a pre-set margin on held-out data, **and** leakage `< τ_sep = 0.15`.
- **KILL threshold (deletes the claim):** leakage `≥ τ_sep`, OR the measured partition fails to beat the random-partition baseline on held-out data. There is no "tune and retry" band: a domain that lands in the inconclusive 0.6–0.8 region is **not admitted** (inconclusive ≠ pass).
- **FAILURE DELETES:** the claim that *this domain is triadic at all.* The domain is **struck from the roster** — not "pending tuning," struck. Honest log entry: "*D* does not factor; F/P/A is the wrong coordinate system here." (This is the per-domain P0 already in TRB's separability test and TRC-P0, generalized across the whole roster.)

### B.2 — TE-P2: β cross-domain transfer (is "interweaving" a method or a metaphor?)

- **Claim under test:** a heuristic learned in domain **X** measurably beats a domain-specific baseline when transferred to domain **Y**, *because* of structural (not surface) isomorphism (β; GSI-RTD §26.4 / Prop 26.1). This is the load-bearing word in the whole vision: **"interweaving."**
- **PRE-REGISTERED PAIR SET (fixed before data, so "systematic" cannot be defined after the fact):** a named list of **N ≥ 10** candidate X→Y domain pairs is fixed in `TE-CHANGELOG` before any scoring. The kill threshold is defined over *this* list, not over a post-hoc convenience subset.
- **Concrete experiment (per pair):** learn the F/P/A impact map in **X** (e.g. TRC, ground truth = DFT/measurement); predict in **Y** (e.g. TRB, held-out atlas/disease) which pillar intervention most improves a target node's `U`; compute β from actual structural overlap; **pre-commit β before scoring the target.**
- **Three arms, equal data budget:** (i) **transfer arm** (target prior seeded from source via β); (ii) **domain-specific baseline** (target-only, no transfer — the bar to beat); (iii) **surface-similarity control** (transfer seeded by label/name similarity instead of structural β — to prove it's *structure*, not vocabulary).
- **Construct-validity arm (NEW — guards §2.5):** β must be shown stable under the embedding-space choice and the subgraph-iso approximation (re-compute β with ≥2 embedding backbones and ≥2 iso surrogates; rank-correlation of β across choices `≥ 0.7`). If β is not stable, it is an uninterpretable composite and the null is confounded.
- **H0 (null):** transfer-arm accuracy ≤ domain-specific baseline (β carries no information).
- **Metric:** top-k weakest-pillar prediction accuracy (or AUC / rank-correlation of predicted-vs-true `ΔU`) on held-out target nodes; report effect size + CI, not just p.
- **PASS threshold:** on the pre-registered pair set, the transfer arm beats the domain-specific baseline by a pre-set margin **and** beats the surface control **and** per-pair β predicts the realized gain (β monotone in measured gain, rank-correlation `≥ ρ_min`) **and** β passes the construct-validity arm. A single lucky pair is not a pass.
- **KILL threshold (deletes "interweaving" — defined numerically so it can fire):** if the **median β-gain CI includes 0 across the pre-registered N pairs**, OR β fails construct validity, OR β-vs-gain rank-correlation `< ρ_min`, then **"interweaving" is deleted** and β is downgraded from heuristic to noise. *Reclassifying failed pairs as "correctly β=0-ed" is explicitly forbidden:* the verdict is computed over the *whole pre-registered list*, so failure cannot be absorbed by quietly dropping the pairs that failed.
- **FAILURE DELETES:** the word **"interweaving"** and the claim that β is a method. Per FM-7, an individual failed pair → `β=0`, flagged non-isomorphic; if the kill threshold above fires, the "cross-domain" adjective is deleted and TE reverts to a **collection of independent single-domain runtimes** that share a score and a runtime but do not transfer.

### B.3 — TE-P3: orchestration value (is the engineer a scientist or a dispatcher?)

- **Claim under test:** the triadic multi-agent stack (TAA F/P/A/Σ jury + weakest-pillar scheduler over triadic-element databases) **beats monolithic / ad-hoc AI at equal budget** (the TMAC/TRC-P10 generalization).
- **H0 (null):** at fixed compute/token/time budget, the triadic-orchestrated stack's task score ≤ a monolithic baseline (no orchestration premium).
- **Equal-budget controls (the whole point):** both arms get **identical** budget on every axis `(T_max, S_max, E_max)`, identical database access, identical eval set; the only variable is *orchestration.* Baselines: (a) one monolithic model, no F/P/A decomposition; (b) ad-hoc multi-agent with no orthogonality and no weakest-pillar gate (to isolate whether it's the *triadic structure*, not "more agents," that helps).
- **Metric:** solution quality per unit budget (Coverage(S)/Cost(S), GSI-RTD §20.6), plus the pre-committed ablation signature.
- **PASS threshold:** the triadic stack beats *both* baselines on score-per-budget by a pre-set margin `μ_min` (CI excludes 0 *and* the lower CI bound `> μ_min`), **and** the ablation signature holds: removing a pillar agent drops SI by `≥ 15%` and removing Σ drops SI by `≥ 25%`.
- **KILL threshold (deletes orchestration value — the near-miss verdict is fixed, not left open):** the claim is **DELETED** if *either* score-per-budget margin CI includes 0 *or* its lower bound `≤ μ_min`; **and** an ablation *near-miss* is adjudicated **as a FAIL, not a pass** — i.e. a pillar-removal drop of, say, 10% (below the 15% threshold) **counts as the ablation signature NOT holding.** There is no "close enough" credit: the thresholds are the verdict, and a value on the failing side of the threshold deletes the claim.
- **FAILURE DELETES:** the claim that orchestration adds scientific value — that the triadic engineer is a *scientist.* "The greatest scientist = the triadic engineer" is removed in full; what remains is "a useful way to organize and dispatch work" — **the dispatcher with no demonstrated premium.**

### B.4 — Symmetric retirement, and why a negative result is NOT a trophy

**The rule, stated before the data so it binds us.** Each falsifier is symmetric: a pass *credits* exactly one grand claim; a fail *deletes* exactly that claim. Failed claims are **removed, not parked.** There is no "promising but needs more work" purgatory where a dead claim quietly keeps its rank — that purgatory is where overclaim hides, and it is forbidden here (the symmetric-retirement rule from TRB §9.8 / TSE).

| Falsifier fails | Grand claim deleted | What survives |
|---|---|---|
| **TE-P1** (a domain) | "that domain is triadic" | the domain is struck; the rest of the meta-layer is unaffected (separability is per-domain) |
| **TE-P2** (kill threshold fires) | "the sciences are *interwoven*; β is a method" | a *collection* of independent single-domain runtimes — shared score & runtime, no transfer |
| **TE-P3** | "the triadic engineer is a *scientist* / the *greatest* scientist" | "an orchestration convention" — useful dispatch, no demonstrated premium. The dispatcher. |

**The fallback, stated once, flatly, so no one can pretend it's a defeat we didn't foresee — and NOT sold:**

> If the falsifiers fail, **Triadic Engineering is not a new science. It is a cross-domain filing-and-dispatch convention** — a consistent way to label, store, and route work across fields using a shared F/P/A vocabulary and a shared runtime. That is useful: a good index with a sane priority queue saves real time and prevents real mistakes. But it sews nothing and discovers nothing the specialists did not. On this fallback the engineer has organized **access** and **set the order of work** — no more.

**The part the symmetric-retirement discipline must guard against — booking a win on every branch.** Earlier drafts of this appendix repeatedly framed a null as "the most valuable thing we can produce, reported as loudly as a win." **That framing is now removed and forbidden,** because it makes the framework *unlosable*: if domains factor, the vision is vindicated; if they don't, "we predicted that, maximally valuable." A program that collects credit on both the pass and the fail branch has stopped being falsifiable — it has only learned to *perform* falsifiability. So the corrected posture is deliberately flat: **a negative result is a fact we report and act on, not a prize we collect.** A clean null does delete an attractive false belief at low cost, and that is genuinely worth having — but "worth having" is not "a win," and the difference is the whole point of pre-registration. A universal decomposition that never reports a non-fit has not been tested; equally, a framework that treats every non-fit as a triumph has not been *risked*. TE must be risked, not just tested.

## 4. Scope, dual-use, the grandiosity guardrail, RH compliance, and explicit NON-GOALS

### 4.1 The grandiosity guardrail, restated — and the under-claiming guardrail beside it

The guardrail against believing our own press is pinned to a *deletable* test, not a reassurance: **the Procrustes guard is TE-P1.** A domain joins the meta-layer only if it actually factors; a domain that fails is **refused entry**, not forced into the boxes anyway. UCT-1 is an argument, not a proof, and confers **no license** to assume any domain is triadic and **no inductive support for the vision** (§A.5). A framework that explains every science equally well explains none of them in particular. **The mirror guardrail, equally binding:** TE must not *under*-claim its control surface either — the layer dispatches the cross-domain work queue (§1.2, §2.4), and describing that as "mere filing" hides a governance surface from scrutiny. Both directions are violations.

### 4.2 RH compliance (binding on this appendix)

- The **vision is B0** (telos; renders no result; cannot be cited as evidence; no UCT warrant).
- The **architecture/composition is B1** (true of the design; verifiable by inspection; modest).
- **Every** "this works / this is the greatest" sentence is **B3-pending** behind TE-P1 / TE-P2 / TE-P3. **No B4 anywhere.** Canon-L2 ceiling; any L3/L4 sentence is a bug.
- This appendix inherits the **dual-use firewall** and the **O5b cross-node worth-ranking ban** (§A.6), both **ungated and never retired** — and names the **two open leaks** (compositional dual-use; intra-node value-frame capture) and the **accountable-human IOU**, none of which are claimed solved.
- The slogan is asserted in the active voice in **exactly one place: the §A.5 B0 VISION block.** Any other active-voice assertion of it is a bug.
- **RUO / not-a-product.** Nothing here is a device, a diagnosis, or a deployable claim.
- **Tone is load-bearing, not decorative — but it is not the brand.** The cynicism *is* the honesty when it names a real limit; it becomes a liability the moment it is *performed* (see §B.4: a null reported "as loudly as a win" is forbidden). "An elegant index with a priority queue" is the worst-case truth we are willing to defend — stated, not sold.

### 4.3 The output lint (refuses, rendering nothing on refusal)

Refuse and render nothing for: any claim above B3; any cross-node U/ℳ-aggregation, leverage-ranking, worth-ranking, or budget triage (type-level); any operational hazardous content or "cheapest-flip" query; any domain rendered into the meta-layer before its TE-P1 gate passes; any "greatest scientist / replaces specialists" sentence outside the **§A.5** B0 VISION block (a *second* active-voice assertion anywhere is a lint failure); any beneficiality/`T_soc` claim lacking a contested value-frame + named accountable authority; **any authoring/agent meta-narration or scaffolding text** (e.g. "I now have everything…", "let me synthesize…") — these carry no claim envelope and are stripped on sight; **any framing of a null result as a "win"/"trophy"/"the real prize"** (§B.4).

### 4.4 Explicit NON-GOALS (the negative mirror)

1. **Not a replacement for specialists** — chemists, biologists, physicians, physicists, economists, regulators, or democratic governance. TE orchestrates and dispatches them; it does not become them.
2. **Not a discovery engine, lab, or theorem-prover** — it produces no ground truth; it sequences calls to the things that do.
3. **Not autonomous** — no closed-loop action on any system, experiment, market, or policy. (But "proposes the work order" is a governance surface, not nothing — §9.)
4. **Not a proof that the sciences are triadic** — separability is a per-domain, contested, *deletable* test (TE-P1), never an assumption; UCT-1 is an argument, not a proof, and motivates nothing inductively.
5. **Not a cross-node resource-allocator** — no ℳ-aggregation or leverage-ranking above the node boundary, in any domain (§A.6(2)). TE cannot decide which person/species/nation/substance gets funded, treated, banned, or saved. (This does **not** close the intra-node value-frame leak — §A.6(3).)
6. **Not a source of operational hazardous content** — no synthesis, route, weaponization, destabilization, diversion, or "cheapest-flip" output anywhere. (This per-query ban does **not** close compositional cross-domain chaining — §A.6(1).)
7. **Not a source of B4 (validated) claims** — that slot is empty by intent; any B4 sentence is a bug.
8. **Not a universal-constant engine** — 0.618, the gate thresholds, β, and every value-frame are tunable, contested, domain-calibrated defaults, not constants of nature.
9. **Not "the greatest scientist"** — that is B0 telos (§A.5), earned only if TE-P1 ∧ TE-P2 ∧ TE-P3 all pass; until then TE is a filing-and-dispatch convention with an excellent index, and we call it that.
10. **Not a solver of "who decides what is beneficial"** — the accountable-human / value-frame governance (§A.6(3)(4)) is an explicit open IOU (§4.5), not a delivered control.
